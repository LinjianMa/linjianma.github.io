Linjian Ma

I am a staff research scientist at Meta Platforms, working on recommendation systems.

Previously, I received my PhD from LPNA and CS Department at UIUC, advised by Edgar Solomonik. Before that, I received my Master's degree from EECS Department at UC Berkeley and I finished my Undergraduate studies from Chu Kochen Honors College at Zhejiang University.

My research interests lie in the intersection of numerical algorithms, high performance computing, system and quantum simulation. In particular, I’m now focusing on developing efficient systems and numerical algorithms for tensor computations with applications in data analytics and quantum simulation. Previously, I worked on optimization and efficient numerical implementation of neural networks.
